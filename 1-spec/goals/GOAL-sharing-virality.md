# GOAL-sharing-virality: Enable sharing and revisiting of readings

**Description**: Allow users who purchased a reading to share it with others and revisit it later. Sharing drives organic acquisition; revisiting increases retention and brand recall.

**Status**: Draft

**Priority**: Could-have

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-sharer](../stakeholders.md)

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Paid readings are accessible via a unique, shareable URL
- [ ] Shared links show a preview (Open Graph meta) that entices the recipient to get their own reading
- [ ] Users can revisit their reading without repaying
