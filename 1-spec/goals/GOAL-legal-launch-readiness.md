# GOAL-legal-launch-readiness: Meet all legal requirements for DACH launch

**Description**: Ensure the landing page is legally compliant for the German-speaking market from day one — DSGVO/GDPR, Impressum, Datenschutz, and cookie consent.

**Status**: Approved

**Priority**: Must-have

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-founder](../stakeholders.md)

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Cookie consent banner blocks non-essential scripts until user opts in
- [ ] Impressum page accessible from all page states, contains legally required information
- [ ] Datenschutzerklarung (privacy policy) covers all data processing, including birth date handling
- [ ] If birth data is sent to FuFirE API, privacy policy discloses this and legal basis is established
- [ ] No third-party tracking fires without prior consent
