# CON-bilingual: German and English from launch

**Category**: Business

**Status**: Active

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-founder](../stakeholders.md)

## Description

The landing page must support both German and English as first-class languages from launch. DACH is the initial target market, but international expansion follows quickly — both languages are standard from day one.

## Rationale

DACH users expect German. International reach requires English. Supporting both from the start avoids a costly retrofit of hardcoded strings later.

## Impact

- All user-facing text must be externalized (i18n system), not hardcoded in components.
- Language detection (browser locale) or manual toggle required.
- Legal pages (Impressum, Datenschutz) need German versions at minimum; English translations recommended.
- Astrology terminology must be reviewed in both languages for correctness.
