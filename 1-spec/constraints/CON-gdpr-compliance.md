# CON-gdpr-compliance: GDPR, Impressum, and Cookie Consent

**Category**: Operational

**Status**: Active

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-founder](../stakeholders.md)

## Description

The landing page must comply with DSGVO/GDPR requirements, include a legally compliant Impressum, and display a cookie consent banner before setting non-essential cookies.

## Rationale

Primary target market is DACH (Germany, Austria, Switzerland). GDPR compliance is legally required. Impressum is mandatory under German TMG/DDG for commercial websites.

## Impact

- Cookie banner must block analytics/tracking scripts until consent is given.
- Impressum and Datenschutz pages (or sections) must be accessible from every page state.
- Birth date input is personal data — if transmitted to an API, privacy policy must disclose this.
- No third-party tracking (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, etc.) without prior consent.
