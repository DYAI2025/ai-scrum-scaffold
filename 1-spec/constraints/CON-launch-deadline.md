# CON-launch-deadline: Launch within two weeks

**Category**: Business

**Status**: Active

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-founder](../stakeholders.md)

## Description

The landing page should be production-ready within approximately two weeks from 2026-04-13 (target: 2026-04-27). The deadline is negotiable but sets the pace for scope decisions.

## Rationale

Founder wants to get the product in front of users quickly. Features that cannot be completed within this window should be deferred to a post-launch iteration.

## Impact

Scope must be tightly prioritized. Must-have features are those required for a viable first impression and conversion flow. Should-have and Could-have features may be cut if they threaten the deadline.
