# CON-no-free-tier: No free readings — paid product only

**Category**: Business

**Status**: Active

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-founder](../stakeholders.md)

## Description

There is no freemium model. All astrology readings (character and partnership) are paid. The landing page must convert visitors into paying customers, not give away the core value for free.

## Rationale

The product's value is in the fused reading itself. Giving it away free would undermine the business model and devalue the astrology fusion.

## Impact

- The landing page flow must build enough trust and intrigue to justify payment before revealing a full reading.
- A paywall or checkout step is required between input and full reveal.
- Sample/preview content (teaser readings, methodology explanation) must demonstrate value without giving away the complete analysis.
- Payment integration (Stripe or similar) is needed.
