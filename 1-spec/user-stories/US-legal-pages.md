# US-legal-pages: Impressum and Datenschutzerklarung

**As a** founder, **I want** the site to include a legally compliant Impressum and Datenschutzerklarung, **so that** the product meets DACH legal requirements from launch.

**Status**: Draft

**Priority**: Must-have

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-founder](../stakeholders.md)

**Related goal**: [GOAL-legal-launch-readiness](../goals/GOAL-legal-launch-readiness.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given I am on any page state, when I look for legal information, then Impressum and Datenschutz links are visible and accessible (e.g., in the footer)
- Given I click Impressum, when the page loads, then it contains all legally required information under German TMG/DDG
- Given I click Datenschutz, when the page loads, then it discloses all data processing activities including birth date handling, payment data, cookies, and any third-party API calls
- Given the site is available in English, when I view legal pages, then at minimum the German version is available (English translation recommended)
