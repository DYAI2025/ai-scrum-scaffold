# US-scroll-journey: Immersive animated scroll journey

**As a** first-time visitor, **I want** to experience a smooth, animated scroll journey through the landing page, **so that** I feel intrigued and trust the product enough to engage with it.

**Status**: Draft

**Priority**: Must-have

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-visitor](../stakeholders.md)

**Related goal**: [GOAL-immersive-experience](../goals/GOAL-immersive-experience.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given I land on the page, when I scroll through the sections, then animations play smoothly (no jank or visual glitches) on desktop and mobile
- Given I am on a pinned section, when I scroll, then the scrub animation responds to my scroll position in real-time
- Given I scroll between pinned sections, when I release, then the page snaps cleanly to the nearest section
